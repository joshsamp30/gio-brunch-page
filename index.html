Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

What are your brunch hours?
We serve brunch every Saturday from 10am – 2pm and Sunday from 10am – 3pm. Last seating is 30 minutes prior to closing. We're closed for brunch on weekdays.
Do I need a reservation?
Reservations are strongly recommended, especially on Sundays. You can book online, call us at 659-299-2787, or fill out the reservation form on this page. Walk-ins are always welcome based on availability.
What is the Bottomless Brunch package?
Our Bottomless Brunch is available every Saturday and Sunday. For $35 per person, enjoy unlimited mimosas, Bloody Marys, or Aperol Spritzes for 2 hours with the purchase of any brunch entrée. It's the perfect way to extend your morning.
Is there a dress code?
We ask that guests dress in smart casual attire. Athletic wear, swimwear, and resort wear are not permitted in the dining room. Our patio has a slightly more relaxed dress code during warmer months.
Can you accommodate large groups or private events?
Absolutely. For parties of 10 or more, we ask that you contact us directly at 659-299-2787 to discuss arrangements. We offer private dining options and custom brunch menus for special celebrations, corporate events, and more.
Do you offer gluten-free or vegetarian options?
Yes — our brunch menu includes several vegetarian and gluten-conscious options. We're happy to accommodate dietary restrictions whenever possible. Please inform your server when you arrive and we'll guide you through the best choices.
Is parking available?
Yes, free parking is available in the Chesterfield Towne Center lot directly adjacent to the restaurant. Parking is plentiful on weekend mornings.
Is patio dining available?
Yes! Our outdoor patio is open seasonally and offers the full brunch menu with complete table service. Patio seating is first-come, first-served and cannot be reserved in advance. On beautiful days, we recommend arriving early to secure a spot.
